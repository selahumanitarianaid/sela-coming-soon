SELA draws its name from a Hebrew word associated with rock, strength, stability, protection, and refuge—a firm place to hold onto in times of crisis.

Its four letters also express the Spanish values that guide our work:
S — Solidaridad (Solidarity)  ·  E — Esperanza (Hope)  ·  L — Lucha (Perseverance)  ·  A — Amor (Love)

SELA exists to sustain, accompany, and mobilize humanitarian aid with compassion, dignity, and action. Our full website is currently under development.
